    How caffeine affects your sleep
    

    Caffeine blocks the signal that makes you feel sleepy. About half is still in your body five hours later and a quarter after ten, though it varies from person to person.
    

    Late caffeine can make it harder to fall asleep, cut deep sleep short and make you wake more often, even if you fall asleep fine. Many people do better stopping about eight hours before bed. Pregnancy, some medicines and some health conditions lower the amount that is safe, so ask your doctor for your own limit.
